Frequently asked questions about Chrisal and the structure of this website.

01Does BrandForge create fake inventory?

No. It can generate collection layouts and product presentation patterns, but real product records, prices, stock, variants, and shipping data must come from the business.

02Can checkout be enabled?

Yes, but only by connecting a real commerce platform or payment system. BrandForge does not pretend a static checkout is functional.

03Can product photography be replaced later?

Yes. Local generated visuals are used as a design foundation and can be replaced with owned product images at any time.

04Can policies be customized?

Yes. Shipping, returns, warranty, privacy, and terms should be updated to match real operations before launch.
